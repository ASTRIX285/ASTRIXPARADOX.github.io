Enhancement

AION 2 Guide, Equipment: Enhancement: the five tabs of the Enhancement window and what to enhance, when.

The Enhancement window

The game's Enhancement window has five tabs, in this order.

  • Growth: Raise the enhance level. Each level adds stats.
  • Manastone: Engrave stones into the item's slots. Accessories take Soulstones.
  • Theostone: Engrave one Theostone, a proc effect, on the weapon.
  • Soul Binding: Raise the item's Soul Binding rate and its bonus lines.
  • Transfer: Move a built-up item's progress onto a better item for the same slot.

Open the Gear Ledger

The Gear Ledger judges each piece you wear against your build and names the next tab to work on.

What to enhance, and when

Enhancement is spent on Unique gear only. Unique gear starts at Lv 35; before that, save Enhance Stones, Kina and manastones. Epic, Rare and Common gear is never enhanced: a Unique piece replaces it. Belt and amulet are the exception: they use their own scrolls, not Enhance Stones, and go to +10 at every grade.

  • Has the Daeva reached the level Unique gear starts at?: No: Save and prepare: enhance nothing but the belt and amulet, and spend the time gathering materials and levelling your crafting, so by Lv 35 you can farm and craft enough to succeed. Yes: Build the Unique pieces, one at a time.
  • Is the piece Unique or better?: No: Do not enhance it, not even with cheap stones. Replace it with a Unique piece. Yes: It is a keeper: follow the steps below.
  • Which piece first?: The weapon, then the guard, then the armour, then the accessories.

The steps for each Unique piece

  • Growth to +10: Every step to +10 succeeds, and extraction gives the Enhance Stones back.
  • Past +10, weapon then guard: Each step can fail and costs a lot; the failure bonus caps the tries. Only the weapon and the guard.
  • Soul Binding: Reroll the lines that are weak for your role first, then Bind towards 100%.
  • Manastones: Top stones for your role in every slot. Only on keepers: Transfer loses them.
  • Theostone: One proc stone on the weapon you keep, last.
  • Transfer: When a better Unique drops for the slot: move the enhance level and Soul Binding across, then rebuild stones and Theostone.
  • Belt and amulet use their own enhance scrolls, not Enhance Stones, and go to +10 at every grade.

Growth

  • Every step up to +10 succeeds.
  • Past +10 each step can fail. The Growth tab shows the chance of the next step.
  • Each failure adds 5 points to the chance of the next try at that step, so every step ends within a known number of tries.
  • A failure costs the attempt, never a level, and never breaks the item.
  • The failure bonus resets after a success.
  • Each try costs Enhance Stones and Kina. Check the cost in the Growth tab.
  • Extracting an item gives its Enhance Stones back, not the Kina.
  • Past +15, Amplified Enhancement on high item level gear uses Amplify Stones. Late game only.

Soul Binding

  • Each item has a Soul Binding rate and 3 or 4 lines, each shown as now and max with a percent. At 100% a line is maxed.
  • Bind costs Kina and Soul Codices. Check the cost in the Soul Binding tab.
  • Each Bind raises the rate by a random amount and never lowers it. It slows near the cap. At 100% the item is locked.
  • Harmonise rerolls one line.
  • Reset rerolls every line and sets the rate back to 0. It needs a Soul Codex: Reset.
  • Fix weak lines before you Bind.
  • A Unique item at 100% can get an extra line. Late game.
  • Whether Soul Codices are the same item as the Soul Binding Book, and what Bind costs at higher rates.

Soul Codices come from: Expeditions, Hidden Cubes, Shugo Festival, Season Shop, Nightmare Shop, Exchange Shop, a few a week for Dream Fragments.

Transfer

  • Gear Transfer moves from a Source Gear to the target and needs Gear Transfer Material.
  • It keeps the enhance level and the Soul Binding lines.
  • It loses the manastones, the Theostone and the extra line.
  • The material is made in Substance Morph.
  • Use it when a better item drops for the same slot. Check the preview first.
  • Exactly what the preview keeps.

Where Enhance Stones come from

  • Daily Dungeon: up to 10,000 a run
  • Ascension Trials: up to 3,500
  • Transcendence: 2,000 a boss
  • Sealed Dungeons: about 1,200 each, once; do the rival faction's too
  • Expeditions: 1,000 a boss
  • Dimensional Invasion: 700 or more a cube
  • Extracting gear
  • Abyss supply
  • Daeva Pass
  • Sealed Wings, morphed into stones in Substance Morph: weekly limit per server, number not confirmed yet

Despite the name, the Daily Dungeon gives 14 attempts a week.

Each Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold pays its completion reward once, then its marker leaves the map. Kina and some items from them, and from field hunting, have a daily limit.
