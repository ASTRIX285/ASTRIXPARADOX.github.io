Combine raises the grade

Each step up is a low chance, so most tries stay the same grade. It pays: Epic hits double Rare, and Unique adds half as much again. Crowd control lasts 1s longer each grade.

  • Rare
  • 11%
  • Epic
  • 9%
  • Unique

How Theostones work

The weapon and the guard each have one Theostone slot.

  • The tab lists your Weapon Theostones, with Combine and Engrave.
  • One Theostone per slot. Engraving a new one replaces the old one.
  • Small value in PvE. Do it last, on the weapon you will keep.
  • The Theostone is lost on Transfer.
  • Only one Theostone's effect applies when identical Theostones are engraved.

Which one for your role

  • DPS: An area element stone for packs, a single target one for bosses.
  • Tank: A Stun or Root stone.

Where they come from

  • Deus Research Base: Theostones
  • Shattered Arkanis: Theostones
