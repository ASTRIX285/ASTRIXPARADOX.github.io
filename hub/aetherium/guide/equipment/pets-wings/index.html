Pets and wings

AION 2 pets and wings: collecting pets, souls for each pet level, the four genera and their stats, wing collections, Superior Daeva Wings and where wings come from.

  • 200 Monster pets
  • 4 Genera
  • 3 Pet levels

Collect them all

Pets are collected, not just worn. Every pet you collect and level gives a small permanent bonus, even when it is not out. The collection is shared by every character on the same server.

Souls raise a pet

A monster pet comes from its souls. These are the totals for each level.

  • 5 souls in all Level 1
  • 30 souls in all Level 2
  • 105 souls in all Level 3

Four genera

Per pet at level 3, for the 200 monster pets on the global servers.

  • Cogni: 41 pets HP +30, Critical Hit +2, Might +1
  • Fera: 65 pets Accuracy Bonus +2, Dexterity +1
  • Natura: 48 pets Critical Hit Resist +2, Precision +1
  • Varian: 46 pets Evasion Bonus +2, Constitution +1
  • A pet's Source tab names the monsters that drop its souls, where they are, and any chest or shop that sells it.
  • Each pet at level 3 adds 1,000 EXP to its genus board, and genus Insight rolls add more stats.
  • Ascension Trial pays Growth Pet Chests, materials for raising pets.
  • Special pets give no owned stat bonus.

Wings

Wings work like pets: the more you own, the more Flight Power you have, worn or not.

  • Superior Daeva Wings
  • Lesser Daeva Wings
  • Intermediate Daeva Wings
  • Aullaeu Wings

Superior Daeva Wings

  • Accuracy Bonus +25
  • HP +400
  • MP +200
  • Flight Power +1,000
  • Defence Bonus +100
  • Front Attack Critical Hit Resist +40

Where wings come from

  • Every Expedition can drop a set of Sealed Wings, a Substance Morph material. Sealed Wings can also be morphed into Enhance Stones, with a weekly limit per server.
  • Sealed Dungeons give materials for wings and statues.
  • The Nightmare shop sells Conqueror Wings for 20,000 Phantasmal Fragments and Nightmare Wings for 160,000, once each.
  • The last main quest, Daeva Trapped in a Nightmare, opens at a full Daeva Gauge and gives the Unique Ultimate Daeva Wings.
