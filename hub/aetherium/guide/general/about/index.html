About AION 2

AION 2 basics: the Elyos and Asmodian factions, the story so far, and Season 1 with its Lv 45 cap on the global servers.

AION 2 is NCSOFT's MMORPG set in the world of Atreia. You play a Daeva, a winged warrior, for one of two factions.

Daevas of light

Elyos

Home: Elysea

The Elyos are the Daevas of light. When the Tower of Aion fell and the Balaur poured into their home of Elysea, their Empyrean Lords gave themselves up to raise a wall of light that sealed the realm away. Siel was lost holding it.

Daevas of shadow

Asmodians

Home: Asmodae

The Asmodians are the Daevas of shadow. When the barrier fell, Asmodae lay open to the Balaur. With Israphel gone, their five remaining Empyrean Lords spent everything to raise the Bulwark that shields their realm.

Picking a faction

  • For good You pick a faction when you make a character. It never changes.
  • The Abyss The only place the two factions meet. Everywhere else they play on separate servers.
  • No mixed parties An Elyos and an Asmodian cannot party together.
  • One-faction Legions A Legion only takes members of its own faction.

To play with friends, pick the same faction and server.

The story so far

  • The war between Elyos and Asmodians ended when the Tower of Aion collapsed. That last battle is remembered as the Final War.
  • With the tower gone, the Dragonkin (the Balaur) broke into the realms of the gods. The surviving gods used the last of their power to raise a barrier, the Veil of Light, which split the world and cut it off.
  • Building it cost them dearly: Siel, guardian of the tower, vanished, and the other Archdeities poured themselves into it until some were destroyed and others faded.
  • Outside the barrier, Atreia became a battlefield. The Dragon Lords Ishtar and Fafnir burned and darkened Poeta.
  • Daevas were hunted and twisted. Under Ishtar, captured Daevas were experimented on and came back as mutants that turn on their own kind. This is the world your Daeva wakes up in.

Season 1

  • Lv 45 Level cap
  • 415 Daevanion points
  • None Heroic items
  • 30 Sept Advanced Access
  • 5 Oct Global launch

Season 1 on the global servers (EU and NA) caps at Lv 45. Korea is at Lv 50, so Korean guides past Lv 45 do not apply here. Season 1 has no Heroic items and gives 415 Daevanion points to spend.

The global servers opened with Advanced Access on 30 September 2026. The full launch followed on 5 October 2026.
