FAQ

Answers to common AION 2 questions about controls, graphics, items, community, account and troubleshooting, each with its source.

Short answers to the questions new and returning players ask most.

Controls and keys

  • I cannot see my mouse cursor. Hold Alt to show it, or switch to AION1 mode, where the cursor is always on screen.
  • How do I change my keys? Open Settings, Key Settings. The General, Menu, Quickslot and Marker tabs hold every key.
  • Expedition and Nightmare have no key. Can I add one? Yes. Give them a key in Settings, Key Settings, Menu.
  • Where should Defiance go? On Space bar, for every class. It breaks crowd control, so keep it off your number keys.
  • Can I swap macro presets with a key? No. There is one macro key, so open the Macro window to change between your 3 presets.
  • Skill use requirements not met keeps popping up with my macro. You cannot move or hide that message. A slightly longer delay between macro skills makes it show less often.

Screen and graphics

  • My whole UI disappeared. You pressed F12. Press it again. If it keeps happening, move Hide UI to another key.
  • My FPS is stuck at 60. Set the Screen Refresh Rate to your monitor's highest and turn Vertical Sync off.
  • How do I see the map while I play? Press Alt + M for a see-through map over the game. Press it again to close it.
  • How do I hide my helmet? In the Closet, click the eye icon next to your helmet.
  • Too many numbers cover the boss attacks. Hide damage numbers by type, or all of them, in Settings, Info Display, Floater.

Items and conveniences

  • Potions get used in the middle of a fight. They are used below 70% HP by default. Change the threshold in Settings, Combat, Auto Use, or press X to turn it off.
  • My bag keeps filling with junk. Set the Looting filter and turn on Auto Extract for the grades you do not want.
  • I lost a good item to extraction. Lock the gear you care about in your inventory: the extract, dismantle and enhance windows look alike.
  • Potions used for you Life Potions are used for you below 70% HP. Lower the threshold in Settings, Combat, Auto Use if you get through too many while questing. X turns it on and off.
  • Looting filter Pick which grades and kinds of item the loot key picks up in Settings, Combat, Looting, so low value items stay out of your bag (the Cube).
  • Auto Extract Auto Extract breaks down equipment of the grades you choose as soon as it drops. It can also take gear you cannot wear or gear under an item level you set. Quick Select marks many items so you can extract them in one go.
  • Lock your gear The extract, dismantle and enhance windows look alike, so it is easy to lose a good piece with one wrong click. Lock the weapons and accessories you care about straight away.
  • Flying further Collecting more wings raises your Flight Power, and owned wings count even when you are not wearing them. To glide further, cancel flight in the air right after a Quick Gliding Dash: you keep your speed as you fall.
  • Characters per account An account starts with 4 character slots and can grow to 8. Some settings are kept per character, so check them after you switch.

Community and account

  • Why can I not trade with another player? Direct trading is turned off on the global servers. Use the Market, which needs Membership.
  • Does Membership cover my whole account? No. It is bought for one character on one server.
  • When are the resets? Daily at 07:00 UTC, weekly on Wednesday at 07:00 UTC. The Reset times page shows them in your own time.
  • Can Elyos and Asmodians group up? No. They play on separate servers and only meet in the Abyss.
  • How do I invite someone nearby to my party? Hold Alt, click their character and pick the party invite. A party holds 5 players and a raid 10.
  • I cannot heal players in the open world. Heals only work on party members, so make a party first.
  • Why do my Daily Dungeon entries drop when I play an alt? Some entry counts are shared by every character on the same server.
  • What is the level cap? Lv 45 on the global servers in Season 1. Guides for Korea go to Lv 50 and do not apply past 45.

Troubleshooting

  • The game will not start. Most crashes come from clashes with other programs. Look up the error code on the Troubleshooting part of the General section.
  • Chat says Please Try Again Later. A known bug. Switch character, go through a loading screen or log in again.
  • I keep getting sent back to server select. After 3 hours with no input the game returns you there. It is not a bug.

First steps

  • Check the official Steam news or Discord for maintenance first.
  • Restart the game and the launcher.
  • Make sure both are fully updated.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • See if the problem is on one character or the whole account.
  • Look at the related setting in game again.

Reporting a problem

  • Write down the exact message or code.
  • Note when and where it happened.
  • Note your platform and the settings involved.
  • Report bugs in the official Discord bug report channel. For account problems, contact NC support.
