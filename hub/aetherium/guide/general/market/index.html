Membership and the Market

Why direct trading is off on the AION 2 global servers, and what Special Quai Membership unlocks: the Market and the Quna Exchange.

On the global servers every sale between players goes through the Market, and the Market needs Membership.

Trading

Direct trading between players is turned off on the global servers, to stop bots. You buy and sell through the Market instead.

Membership

The Market and the Quna Exchange need Special Quai Membership. Membership is bought for one character on one server, not for the whole account, so put it on the character you play most. The Daeva Pass is also bought per character.

Buying for less

In the Market, sort by price per unit, not by the price of the whole stack. A big stack can look cheap and still cost more for each item.

Entries shared by server

Some entry counts, such as Daily Dungeon entries, are shared by every character on the same server. Using them on an alt also uses them up for your main there.
