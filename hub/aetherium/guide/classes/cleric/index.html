Healer Healer

Main role New player friendly: Very high

  • Earth's Retribution MP filler.
  • Light of Regeneration Unlocks at Lv 7. Party heal over time, usable on the move.
  • Condemnation Unlocks at Lv 8. Your main damage skill.
  • Chain of Torment Unlocks at Lv 4. Opens Condemnation, and the target takes more damage from the whole party.
  • Healing Light Unlocks at Lv 10. Heals you and the lowest-health ally at 40 m.
  • Radiant Recovery Unlocks at Lv 12. Party heal that also cleanses.

DPS Damage

New player friendly: High

Damage first, through Chain of Torment and Condemnation, with your own heals to stay up. Heals still grow with Attack.

  • Chain of Torment Unlocks at Lv 4. Opens Condemnation, and the target takes more damage.
  • Judgment Thunder Always ready: it fills every gap in stack A.
  • Divine Aura Unlocks at Lv 3. Free damage every 30 s.
  • Bolt Unlocks at Lv 14. Your charged burst.
  • Healing Light Unlocks at Lv 10. Keeps you up between pulls.
  • Prayer of Amplification slot Lv 27
  • Noble Aura slot Lv 32
  • Earth Punishment slot Lv 37
