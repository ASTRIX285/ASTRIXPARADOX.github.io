Classes

AION 2 classes: the class roles table, every class at a glance, which class suits each role and how to choose one.

The eight classes

  • Gladiator
  • Templar
  • Assassin
  • Ranger
  • Sorcerer
  • Spiritmaster
  • Cleric
  • Chanter

Class roles

  • Class, Tank, Healer, Support, DPS
  • Gladiator, A tier, -, -, A tier
  • Templar, S tier, -, -, B tier
  • Assassin, -, -, -, A tier
  • Ranger, -, -, -, A tier
  • Sorcerer, -, -, -, A tier
  • Spiritmaster, -, -, -, A tier
  • Cleric, -, S tier, -, C tier
  • Chanter, -, A tier, Main role, B tier

Main role: the role the class's main build plays. Can play: we have a build for it. -: no build for that role.

Which class for which role

  • Tank: Main role for Templar. Also played by Gladiator. Easiest start: Templar (new player friendly: very high).
  • Healer: Main role for Cleric. Also played by Chanter. Easiest start: Cleric (new player friendly: very high).
  • Support: Main role for Chanter. Easiest start: Chanter (new player friendly: medium).
  • DPS: Main role for Gladiator, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster. Also played by Templar, Cleric, Chanter. Easiest start: Templar (new player friendly: very high).

By activity

  • Solo and levelling: Gladiator: Solo or levelling. Templar: Offensive tank (solo and AoE). Chanter: Solo and AoE.
  • Party: main tank: Gladiator: Main tank, no Templar. Templar: Shield tank.
  • Party: off-tank next to a Templar: Gladiator: Off-tank, with a Templar.

Choosing a class

  • Pick the job you enjoy. Holding the boss (Tank), keeping the party alive (Healer), making everyone stronger (Support) or dealing the damage (DPS). The table above shows who plays what.
  • New to AION 2? Our build data rates these main builds the friendliest to learn: Gladiator, Templar, Cleric.
  • Check the class page. Each one lists its builds by role, the key skills, the stat order and the manastones, with links into your Ascent Plan.
  • Link your Daeva. Find your character on the Daeva Card and the class page shows where your setup differs from the build, and what that costs you. It never stops you playing your way.
