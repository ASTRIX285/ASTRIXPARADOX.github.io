Professions

AION 2 Guide, Crafting: Professions: the five crafting professions, what each makes and how a craft works.

The five professions

One character can learn all five crafts. Each one levels on its own, and every craft you try gives proficiency, success or not.

  • Blacksmithing: Makes Greatswords Longswords Daggers Maces Guards Refines Orichalcum Ingots
  • Armorsmithing: Makes Helm Pauldrons Breastplate Gloves Greaves Boots Cloak Refines Leathers
  • Handicrafting: Makes Rings Earrings Necklaces Bracelets Bows Staffs Refines Wood and gem decorations
  • Alchemy: Makes Spellbooks Orbs Potions Serums Scrolls Manastones Soulstones Refines Magic Crystals, papers and powders
  • Cooking: Makes Buff food Drinks

Crafting is free to use. Membership gates the Market, not crafting.

Proficiency

Crafting opens at about Lv 10, at the Crafting Table in each town's crafting area.

How a craft works

  • Two gauges, success and failure, fill turn by turn. The first one full decides the result.
  • The higher your proficiency above the recipe, the better the chance. The recipe window shows the chance.
  • Fail A failure uses up all the materials.
  • Hit or move Being hit or moving during a craft counts as a failure.
  • Core Gear crafts never reach 100%, except recipes that use a Core: those always succeed and give a bound item.
  • Combo A successful gear craft can trigger a combo that gives the Splendent version, one grade higher.

Which first: our pick for your class

  • Handicrafting first: accessories fill five slots and gain most from the extra Soul Binding line on crafted gear.
  • Alchemy first, gathering Odyle alongside, then the weapon and the Guard. Good Manastones sell well.
  • Armorsmithing last for PvE; crafted armour may matter more in PvP.
  • Cooking is cheap and useful early.
