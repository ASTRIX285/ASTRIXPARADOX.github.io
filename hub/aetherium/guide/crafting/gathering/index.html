Gathering

AION 2 Guide, Crafting: Gathering: the materials you gather, their qualities and where to find them.

What you gather

Gathering (essence extraction) feeds every craft. Gather as you quest: it levels at the same time.

  • Ore: Orichalcum Ore Used for Ingots for Blacksmithing and Armorsmithing Blacksmithing Armorsmithing
  • Wood: Yggdrasil Log Elyos Asvata Log Asmodian Used for Bows and staffs Handicrafting
  • Gems: Sapphire Gemstone Diamond Gemstone Ruby Gemstone Used for Jewellery decorations and Alchemy powders Handicrafting Alchemy
  • Herbs: Aria Elyos Targena Elyos Azpha Asmodian Calendula Asmodian Used for Alchemy serums and potions Alchemy
  • Cooking ingredients: Mela Elyos Cypri Elyos Raydam Asmodian Pujery Asmodian Used for Cooking Cooking
  • Odyle: Used for Nearly every gear recipe

Four qualities

  • Base
  • Fine
  • Pure
  • Radiant

Most materials come in four qualities: Base, Fine, Pure and Radiant. A recipe asks for the matching quality.

Material Conversion turns a higher quality into a lower one, never up.

Good to know

Odyle floats in the air near camps, towns and points of interest. You need wings, and running out of wing stamina stops the extraction.

Gathering skills include Search (shows nodes on the map), Bountiful Harvest (double yield) and Appraiser (higher quality). Resetting them costs 50,000 Kina.

Each node has 2 to 4 uses, with a lockout per spot. Gathering has its own success and failure gauges.

Verteron and Altgard have the most nodes. Odyle is also found in Chaotic Lower Reshanta.

Raw Leather comes from certain monsters only, never from nodes.

Each profession's merchant sells the additives: Solvent, Catalyst, Spiritstones, Glass Bottles, Abrasive, Sandpaper and condiments.
