AION 2 dungeons at a glance: players, entry cycle, main rewards, level and item level for every Expedition, Transcendence, Nightmare, Ascension Trial and more.

At a glance

Item level is the game's entry gate. Where a dungeon has two difficulties the table shows both, easiest first. Many groups ask for more than the minimum.

  • Vakron Sky Island Expedition, 2 to 5, Spend Odyle Energy on the reward, Unique armour and a Guard (item Lv 70), 45, 1,400

Kinds of content

  • Expedition: Players 1 to 5. Cycle Spend Odyle Energy on the reward. Expedition gear and Kina Krao Cave Draupnir Urugugu Canyon Vakron Sky Island Fire Temple Ferocious Horn Den
  • Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds: Players 1. Cycle Once each. Daevanion Crystals, Enhance Stones, Noble Belt scrolls Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds

  • Shugo Festival: Players Varies. Cycle Daily keys. Centuryroot Tokens, growth materials Shugo Festival
