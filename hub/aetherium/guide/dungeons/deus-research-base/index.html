Deus Research Base

AION 2 Deus Research Base: how to enter, difficulties, rewards, its reset cycle and short boss notes, with a check for your own Daeva.

A Transcendence dungeon and the only home of Compass and Bell Arcana. Watch the drops along the route and the chain binds of the final boss.

Can you enter?

Link a Daeva on the Daeva Card for an automatic check, or type a level and item level here.

No Daeva linked Link a Daeva to check whether you can enter.

Stages

No character level is listed for Transcendence: item level is the gate.

  • Stage, Level, Item level, Arcana, Effect
  • Stage 1, Not listed, 1,600+, Common to Rare, None
  • Stage 2, Not listed, 1,900+, Common to Epic, Balance: Strong elites leave a zone when they die that boosts monsters and hurts you.
  • Stage 3, Not listed, 2,200+, Common to Unique, Rage: Monsters that are not bosses hit harder below half HP.
  • Stage 4, Not listed, 2,500+, Common to Unique One Unique Arcana guaranteed, Judgment: Boss hits stack a mark that explodes for damage to those near you.

Each clear gets a Rank from time, revives, Kisks used and monsters killed. Monsters killed after the final boss spawns do not count.

What it rewards

Why you go Arcana (Compass, Bell, Chalice), Theostones, Manastones and Soulstones

Arcana slots here: Compass, Bell, Chalice. Compass and Bell drop only in this dungeon.

Where to get each piece of gear

Weekly cycle

Every Stage is open from the start. Kina from runs drops after the first 55 clears in a week; the count resets on Wednesday.

Weekly reset

Weekly entries and reward limits reset every Wednesday at 07:00 UTC.

Boss notes

  • Gelcos Mid boss Take the left path at the first fork.
  • Nazmun Mid boss Further along the same path.
  • Atiel Final boss Kill the set number of monsters first. Its chains bind players together: hold position until they break, or move away from the centre player when the whole party is bound.
