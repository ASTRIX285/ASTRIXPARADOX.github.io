Ascension Trial

AION 2 Ascension Trial: how to enter, difficulties, rewards, its reset cycle and short boss notes, with a check for your own Daeva.

A solo timed run through rooms to a final boss, scored at the end. Side objectives buff you or weaken the boss. The dungeon changes each week.

Can you enter?

Link a Daeva on the Daeva Card for an automatic check, or type a level and item level here.

No Daeva linked Link a Daeva to check whether you can enter.

Difficulties

  • Difficulty, Level, Item level, Notes
  • Easy, 45, 1,000+, 5 deaths allowed, 18 minutes.
  • Normal, 45, 1,500+, 3 deaths allowed, 16 minutes.
  • Hard, 45, 2,000+, 2 deaths allowed, 14 minutes.
  • Extreme, 45, 2,500+, 2 deaths allowed, 13 minutes. Wrath: monsters below half HP hit harder.

The dungeon changes each week between Nightmare Altar and Sanctum of Loathing. Check the in game menu for this week's.

What it rewards

Why you go Enhance Stones, Unique Amplify Stone Fragments, Growth Pet Chests and Ascension Binding Chests

  • Ascension Trial Lv [object Object]

Where to get each piece of gear

Weekly cycle

Three entries a week. An entry is spent the moment you go in, win or lose, so pick a difficulty you can finish.

Weekly reset

Weekly entries and reward limits reset every Wednesday at 07:00 UTC.
