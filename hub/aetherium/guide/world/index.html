World

AION 2 world: zones, the Abyss, Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds, the Spacetime Rift and events such as the Shugo Festival.

Where things are in AION 2: the zones, the Abyss, the exploration spots on the map and the timed events.

  • Zones The zones you level through.
  • Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds The question marks on your map.
  • The Abyss Where the two factions meet.
  • Crossing to the other faction The Spacetime Rift.
  • Events The Shugo Festival and the other timed events.

Zones

  • Base of Dawn: Cantas Valley, Verteron The Dawn Legion's hidden command base, tucked in a gorge of Cantas Valley.
  • Cantas Valley: Verteron Once a supply point rich in Odium for the Daevas, left empty when the Odium ran out. The Dawn Legion now draws the last of its Aether with collectors.
  • Tolbas Forest: Forest that hides Urugugu Canyon, sacred land of the Aulau tribe. Urugugu Canyon
  • Other zones Pending: The other zones are not listed yet.

Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds

  • Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds: Question marks on the map in both factions' lands. Clear them for Daevanion Crystals and Noble Belt scrolls and to fill the Daeva Gauge. Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds

The Abyss

  • The Abyss: A PvP zone. The entrance to Draupnir lies inside it, so expect players of the other faction on the way in. Draupnir

Crossing to the other faction

  • Spacetime Rift: Opens every three hours for about ten minutes and takes you into the other faction's land, where you can clear their Sealed Dungeons, Strongholds and regional quests. PvP is on there.

Events

  • Shugo Festival: Opens every hour on the hour from the Shugo icon beside the minimap. Shugo Festival
  • Dimensional Invasion Pending: A separate event that opens at half past each hour. More detail is not listed yet.
