Skills

How AION 2 skills level up for every class: the Mastery cap, Specialty perks, Daevanion skill nodes and stigmas.

How skill levels work for every class. Your own class's skills, and where to put them, are on its class page and in the Ascent Plan.

  • Skills by class Pick your class to open its page.
  • Mastery How far Mastery takes a skill, and what lifts it higher.
  • Specialty picks The perks a skill opens as it levels.
  • Skill bar and macro Lay out your bar and set up your macros.

Mastery cap

Mastery takes an active skill to Lv 11 and a passive skill to Lv 10 at most. Levels above that come from Daevanion skill nodes (+1 each), gear stat lines and Arcana.

Daevanion skill nodes (+1 each) gear stat lines Arcana

Specialty perks

  • Every active skill has 5 Specialty perks.
  • Skill Lv 8 opens three perks and your first perk slot.
  • Skill Lv 12 opens a fourth perk and a second slot.
  • Skill Lv 16 opens the fifth perk.
  • Skill Lv 20 opens the third slot, so you use three of the five.
  • Skill Lv 8 3 perks open, first perk slot
  • Skill Lv 12 4th perk, second slot
  • Skill Lv 16 5th perk
  • Skill Lv 20 third slot

Skill nodes on the Daevanion boards

Each core skill has one +1 level node on each of the first four Daevanion boards: Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel and Triniel. The fifth board, Azphel, has none.

Stigmas

Stigmas open at Lv 22 through the quest The One Who Hears the Voice. You get one stigma slot at Lv 22 and one more at Lv 27, 32 and 37, four in all. Stigma Specialty perks open at stigma Lv 5, 10, 15 and 20.

How skills level up

Skills are raised in the Mastery window. What it costs to level a skill is not confirmed for the global servers yet.
