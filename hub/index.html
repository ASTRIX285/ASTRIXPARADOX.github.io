The ASTRIX PARADOX mission

Gaming is better with
an intelligent partner.

ASTRIX PARADOX is building AI gaming companions that help players understand their character, gear, builds, progress and choices in one clear place. The goal is not to play the game for you. It is to give you the information and insight to play it your way.

Destiny 2 is our first proving ground. Every tool that follows will be built around its own game, its own systems and the way its community plays.
