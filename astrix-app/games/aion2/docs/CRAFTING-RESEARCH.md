# AION 2 crafting and gathering: research for The Aetherium (11 Oct 2026)

Purpose: the facts a Crafting section of The Aetherium would rest on, each with a status, plus what is still unknown and the in-game checks Miguel can do. Sources stay in the backend only (never named on a page). Nothing here is copied from another site's layout or text; database sites are used to cross-check facts only, never scraped.

Status key:
- CONFIRMED: two or more independent sources agree, or NCSOFT's own guidebook or probability page (via a cited source).
- REPORTED: one source only.
- CONFLICT: sources disagree. Needs an in-game check.
- KR ONLY: Korean or Taiwanese service; global not confirmed.

## 1. What the public character data gives us

- The official character data we read (search, character, item, Daevanion) has no crafting or gathering levels, no materials and no inventory. Checked against our EU fixtures: no craft, gather or profession fields. (CONFIRMED, our own data)
- What we can see: every worn item's name, grade and enchant level. So we can tell if a Daeva wears crafted gear (Dragon Lord, Orichalcum, Yggdrasil/Asvata lines) and what a crafted upgrade for that slot would be.
- Anything about the player's own crafting has to be typed in by the player (like the Daevanion points box) and saved on their device.

## 2. Professions (global)

Five crafting professions, all learnable on one character, each levelled on its own. (CONFIRMED: Corpus, Timesaver, The Games Wiki, aion2.run)

| Profession | Makes | Refines (its own materials) |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | Greatswords, longswords, daggers, maces, Guards | Orichalcum Ingots, Balaur horn materials |
| Armorsmithing | Helm, pauldrons, breastplate, gloves, greaves, boots, cloak | Strong/tough leather, Balaur leathers |
| Handicrafting | Rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, bows, staffs | Wood, gem decorations, Balaur scales |
| Alchemy | Spellbooks, orbs, potions, serums, scrolls, Manastones, Soulstones | Magic Crystals, papers, powders |
| Cooking | Buff food and drinks (no gear) | none |

- Names differ between sites (Weaponsmithing/Tailoring/Jewelcrafting on some). Those look like translation differences. Use the global client names above. (CONFLICT on naming only; check the in-game menu)
- Recipe counts in the global client: about 484 recipes, 247 materials (REPORTED, aion2.run). Elyos and Asmodian have separate versions of most recipes. (REPORTED, The Games Wiki)

## 3. Unlock and access

- Crafting is free to use. The Membership gates the Market and the Kina to Quna exchange, not crafting. (REPORTED, Timesaver)
- Crafting opens at about character Lv 10 (REPORTED, Timesaver). Menu shortcut given as Esc then Crafting (Timesaver) or Alt + G (Playnews). (CONFLICT, check in game)
- Crafting happens at the Crafting Table in each town's crafting area. (CONFIRMED)
- Essence extraction (gathering) unlock: one source says from the start (ExitLag), another says character Lv 45 since a Korean change on 28 Jan 2026 (The Games Wiki). (CONFLICT, Miguel can check at Lv 41)

## 4. Proficiency (crafting level)

- Novice 1 to 50, then Professional (or Expert) 1 to 50 after a promotion quest. Global data uses one 1 to 100 scale for recipe requirements. (CONFIRMED)
- Every craft gives proficiency, success or not. (CONFIRMED, NC guidebook via The Games Wiki)
- Korean service raised Expert to Lv 70 in April 2026. (KR ONLY)

## 5. How a craft works

- Two gauges, success and failure, fill turn by turn. The first full one decides the result. (CONFIRMED)
- A failure uses up all the materials. Being hit, or moving, during a craft counts as a failure. (CONFIRMED)
- Higher proficiency than the recipe level raises the chance. The recipe window shows your expected chance, and a toggle shows the combo chance. (CONFIRMED)
- Official sample chances (NC probability page, 7 Oct 2026, via The Games Wiki): Orichalcum Greatsword 74.0% at gap 0, 93.1% at gap 50; True Dragon Lord Longsword 67.5% and 94.6%. (CONFIRMED: NC figures, region not stated)
- Gear crafts cannot reach 100%, except recipes that use a Core, which always succeed and give a bound item. (CONFIRMED for Core in KR notes; global REPORTED)
- Combo: a successful gear craft can trigger a combo (25% on the examples) that gives the Splendent version, one grade higher. (CONFIRMED)

## 6. The crafted gear ladder

Each step uses the Splendent version of the previous step, so steps 1 to 3 only move on with a combo. A non-Splendent result is a dead end: use it or sell it. (CONFIRMED)

| Step | Weapon example (Elyos / Asmodian) | Proficiency needed |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Orichalcum Greatsword; Yggdrasil / Asvata Bow | 3 |
| Expert's | Expert's ... | 15 |
| Artisan's | Artisan's ... | 30 |
| Dragon Lord | True Dragon Lord / Star Dragon Lord | 55 |
| Transfer step 1 | White Dragon Lord / Dark Dragon Lord | Transfer crafting |
| Transfer step 2 | Wise Dragon Lord / Ebony Dragon Lord | Transfer crafting |

- Guards need 5, 20, 35, 60. Boots and cloaks 10, 25, 40, 60. Bracelets combine three of the previous bracelet. (REPORTED, The Games Wiki)
- Bow item levels (Splendent in brackets): 18 (22), 28 (35), 39 (44), 62 (70), 78 (86), 94 (102). (REPORTED, Corpus)
- Accessory bases: rings Sapphire, earrings Diamond, necklaces Ruby. (CONFIRMED)
- Transfer crafting keeps the base item's enhancement, amplification and Soul Binding levels, binds the new item to you, and keeps the transfer piece if the craft fails (the other materials are lost). (CONFIRMED)
- Whether White/Wise (Dark/Ebony) Dragon Lord are in the global client: aion2.run lists them in global data at item level 102; aion2hub says "not in the Global client as of 19 Sep". (CONFLICT)
- Grades: Common, Rare, Epic, Unique. Heroic is not in the global launch; global Dragon Lord pieces are Unique. (REPORTED by two sources)

## 7. Why crafted gear matters

- Crafted gear has one more Soul Binding line than dungeon drops (Dragon Lord: 5 lines, drops 3 to 4). (CONFIRMED)
- Crafted weapon adds PvE Damage +5% (REPORTED, Codexgames).
- Soul Binding rerolls stats with Kina and Soul Codex (accessories use Soulstones). Reset rerolls every line; Sync rerolls one. Soul-bound items cannot be traded. (CONFIRMED)
- Costs given: about 30,000 Kina plus a Soul Codex per bind, about 1,000,000 Kina for the extra slot. (REPORTED, community figures)

## 8. Materials and where they come from

### Gathered (essence extraction)

| Type | Global names | Qualities | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ore | Orichalcum Ore | Base, Fine, Pure, Radiant | Ingots for Blacksmithing and Armorsmithing |
| Wood | Yggdrasil Log (Elyos), Asvata Log (Asmodian) | same | Bows and staffs |
| Gem | Sapphire, Diamond, Ruby Gemstone | same | Jewellery decorations, Alchemy powders |
| Herb | Aria, Targena (Elyos); Azpha, Calendula (Asmodian), plus Fresh versions | Base, Fresh | Alchemy serums and potions |
| Cooking | five per faction, e.g. Mela, Cypri (Elyos); Raydam, Pujery (Asmodian) | one | Cooking |
| Odyle | Odyle | Base, Fine, Pure, Radiant | Nearly every gear recipe, matching quality |

(CONFIRMED: The Games Wiki and aion2.run agree)

- Where: Verteron and Altgard have the most nodes, then Eltnen and Morheim (not open on global at launch), and Chaotic Lower Reshanta for Odyle. (REPORTED, aion2.run node counts)
- Odyle floats in the air near camps, towns and points of interest; you need wings, and running out of wing stamina cancels the extraction. (REPORTED, Playnews)
- Odyle also drops from Expedition and Conquest bosses (15 per five-player party on Normal or Hard since July 2026, KR notes). (KR ONLY)
- Gathering has its own two gauges, nodes have 2 to 4 uses, and there is a per-spot lockout. (CONFIRMED)
- Gathering skills: shared ones (Deft Hands, Delicate Touch, Goddess of Fortune) and per type (Search shows nodes on the map, Bountiful Harvest for double yield, Appraiser for higher quality). Reset costs 50,000 Kina. (CONFIRMED on the tree; names provisional)
- Promotion to Expert gathering needs a quest; one source says it needs a Splendent Ruby. (REPORTED)
- Material Conversion turns higher quality into lower quality, never up. (REPORTED, KR notes)

### Bought

- Each profession's crafting merchant sells the additives: Orichalcum Solvent, Catalyst, Spiritstones, Glass Bottle, Abrasive, Sandpaper, cooking condiments. (CONFIRMED)

### Refined from gathered (first step in each profession)

- Orichalcum Ingot = 2 ore + Solvent. Wood = 2 logs + Sandpaper. Gem Decoration = 2 gems + Abrasive. Magic Crystal = 2 Spiritstones + Catalyst. Life Serum = Fresh Aria + Glass Bottle. (REPORTED, aion2.run; ratios to confirm in game)

### Dropped

- Raw Leather: from certain monsters only, not nodes; drop rate raised in Chaotic Lower Reshanta, Verteron and Altgard on 7 Oct 2026 (global). (CONFIRMED)
- Balaur horn, scale and leather materials: made from Balaur's Essence (KR change, July 2026). Balaur's Essence drops in the Ludra raid. (REPORTED)
- Wrathful Mind, Will and Ego (Dragon Lord and transfer steps): boss and dungeon drops, best from the Ludra raid (10% each); Wrathful Ego also in the Season Shop for Oath Coin, 10 per account. (REPORTED, allthings.how; boss levels quoted as 45 to 75, which does not fit the global level cap of 45, so likely mixed regions: CONFLICT)
- Spiritstones: monsters, dungeons and the Abyss, as well as the merchant. (REPORTED)
- Artisan's Ultimate Refining Stone: Shugo shop from Lv 45, and recipes. (REPORTED)
- Soul Codex: dungeons, Expeditions, Dimensional Invasion, Shugo Festival, Nightmare Shop, Hidden Cubes, Trade Shop. (REPORTED)

## 9. What players say to do first (opinion, never stated as fact)

- Handicrafting first: accessories fill five slots and gain most from crafted Soul Binding. (Corpus, Timesaver)
- Alchemy first, with Odyle gathering alongside, then weapon and Guard. Superior Manastones sell well. (Codexgames)
- Armorsmithing last for PvE; crafted armour may matter more for PvP. (Corpus, Codexgames)
- Gather all the time while levelling. Do not soul-bind anything you might sell. Level a profession on cheap low recipes.
- Cooking is cheap and useful early (for example a Physical Damage dish).

The Aetherium should show these as "What players do" with the reason, not as a rule, until Miguel picks a recommended order.
