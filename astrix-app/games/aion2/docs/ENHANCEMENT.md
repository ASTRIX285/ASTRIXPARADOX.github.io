# AION 2 gear Enhancement: how to guide a player (research record, 17 Oct 2026)

The game's Enhancement window has five tabs: Growth, Manastone, Theostone, Soul Binding, Transfer. This record says what each does, what our official character data already tells us per item, and the order to advise.
Status: CONFIRMED (Miguel's in-game screenshots, 17 Oct 2026, or the official character data), REPORTED (guides agree), UNKNOWN. Cost tables from datamined client files are not shown on pages; only costs Miguel captured, or "check the cost in game".

## 0. What the official item data gives us per item (CONFIRMED, lv45-items fixture)
enchantLevel and maxEnchantLevel; magicStoneSlotCount and magicStoneStat (the manastones engraved); godStoneSlotCount (Theostone); soulBindRate; subStats (Soul Binding lines, value and minValue); subSkills (skill +1 lines); grade.
Slot counts seen: Unique 4 manastone slots, data "Legend" (game Epic) 3, Rare 2, belt and amulet none.
So the Gear page can give each item its exact next step.

## 1. Growth (enhance level)
- CONFIRMED in game: +11 to +12 on Unique gloves shows 50% success; "Success Chance increases by 5.00% on failure"; costs Enhance Stones and Kina (3,750 stones and 188,000 Kina on Miguel's gloves); each level adds Defence and HP on armour (+11 Defence, +9 HP on that step).
- CONFIRMED (our record, 11 Oct): +1 to +10 always succeed; then 65, 50, 35, 25, 20 percent; failure never drops a level. Caps: Common and Rare +5 (accessories +10), game Epic +10, Unique +15; belt and amulet +10.
- With +5% per failure, the most tries a step can take is: +11 8, +12 11, +13 14, +14 16, +15 17 (worked out from the confirmed rates; matches metabot).
- Whether the bonus resets after a success: REPORTED yes (GameWith KR). In-game check.
- No protection item found; nothing breaks or drops (gear). REPORTED.
- Extraction gives the Enhance Stones back, not the Kina. REPORTED full refund up to +10.
- Enhance Stone sources (REPORTED, games.gg): Daily Dungeon (up to 10,000 a run), Ascension Trials (up to 3,500), Transcendence (2,000 a boss), Sealed Dungeons (about 1,200 each, once; do the rival faction's too), Expeditions (1,000 a boss), Dimensional Invasion (700+ a cube), extraction, Abyss supply, Daeva Pass. Sealed Wings morph into stones in Substance Morph, weekly limit per server (number UNKNOWN).
- Above +15: Amplified Enhancement on high item level gear, own rates and Amplify Stones (REPORTED, late game only).

## 2. Manastone (and Soulstone on accessories)
- CONFIRMED in game: the tab lists one line per slot ("Manastone engraving available"); materials "Manastone / Soulstone"; buttons Engrave and Auto Engrave.
- REPORTED: Engrave rolls stones into the slots; no failure or breaking; Auto Engrave repeats until a chosen stat appears and can overwrite good lines. Whether one Engrave rerolls every slot or fills one: in-game check (Miguel's screen shows a line per slot).
- Top values (REPORTED, KR data): HP 160, Defence 100, Block 20, Attack 10, Crit and Accuracy 15, Damage Boost family 100 (= 1%). Damage Boost family only on the top tier.
- By role (REPORTED): DPS Front/Back Damage Boost, then Weapon or Critical Damage Boost, then Damage Boost, then Attack. Gladiator: Front Damage Boost. Tank: HP, Defence, Block and Accuracy until you stop dying, then damage.
- Lost on Transfer (REPORTED). Only put good stones on keeper gear.

## 3. Theostone (weapon only)
- CONFIRMED in game: "Weapon Theostone" list, Combine and Engrave.
- REPORTED: a weapon proc, one per weapon (17 effects, Rare, Epic, Unique). Engraving replaces the old one. Combine upgrades grade at a low chance (Rare to Epic about 11%, Epic to Unique about 9%); input count UNKNOWN.
- Small value in PvE (REPORTED). DPS: AoE element for packs, single-target for bosses. Tank: Stun or Root. Do it last, on the final weapon. Lost on Transfer.

## 4. Soul Binding
- CONFIRMED in game: each item has a Soul Binding Rate; 3 to 4 lines each shown as current/max with a percent (e.g. Accuracy 21/28); skill lines (+1) too; at 100% a line is maxed. Bind costs Kina (30,000 on Miguel's gloves at 45%, 42,000 on his breastplate at 80.9%) plus Soul Codices (10 and 12 needed). "Insufficient Soul Codices" blocks it.
- REPORTED: each Bind raises the rate by a random amount, never lowers it, slower near the cap; at 100% the item is locked. Harmonise (Sync) rerolls one line; Reset rerolls all and sets the rate to 0 (needs Soul Codex: Reset). Fix bad lines before you Bind.
- Codex sources (REPORTED): Expeditions, Hidden Cubes, Shugo Festival, Season Shop, Nightmare Shop; Exchange Shop 5 per week for Dream Fragments.
- Extra line at 100% on Unique (Philosopher's Stones plus 1,000,000 Kina; REPORTED, late game).
- Best lines (REPORTED, Gladiator): weapon Weapon Damage Boost, Combat Speed, Might, Precision; Damage Boost only on weapon and chest; Combat Speed only on gloves. Tank: Block, HP, Accuracy (Miguel's guard rolled Block 37/37).

## 5. Transfer
- CONFIRMED in game: Gear Transfer moves from a Source Gear to the target and needs Gear Transfer Material.
- REPORTED: moves the enhance level and Soul Binding lines; loses manastones, Theostone and the extra line. Material made in Substance Morph. Use it to move a built-up piece onto a better item of the same slot. Check the preview first.

## 6. The order to advise (superseded in part by section 9, Miguel, 18 Oct 2026)
1. Before Lv 35 (no Unique gear yet): enhance nothing but the belt and amulet (own scrolls). Save Enhance Stones, Kina and manastones; gather and level crafting instead (section 9).
2. Epic, Rare and Common gear: never enhanced. A Unique piece replaces it.
3. Each Unique piece: Growth to +10 first (always succeeds, stones refundable). Weapon first, then guard, then armour.
4. Past +10: weapon, then guard, one step at a time; the pity bonus means each step ends within a known number of tries.
5. Soul Binding on keepers: reroll bad lines first, then Bind.
6. Top manastones for your role on keepers only.
7. Theostone on the final weapon.
8. New better Unique for the same slot: Transfer, then rebuild manastones and Theostone.

## 9. The decision logic: what to enhance, and when (Miguel, in game, 18 Oct 2026)

Miguel's rulings: do not recommend enhancing even Epic gear; Unique gear does not drop before Lv 35; before then, players should gather materials and level their crafting (armour, weapons, food, jewellery) so that by the time they are proficient they can farm the resources to build enough material to succeed. Data: progression.json unique-gear-level (35); enhancement.json logic and order.

### Gate 1: the level
- Below Lv 35: no Unique gear exists for the Daeva yet, and everything worn (Common, Rare, Epic) will be replaced.
  - Do not spend Enhance Stones, Kina or manastones on gear.
  - Belt and amulet are the exception: their own scrolls, not Enhance Stones, +10 at every grade.
  - Prepare instead: gather all the time (Orichalcum Ore, wood, gems, herbs, Odyle) and level the five crafting professions. Every craft gives proficiency, success or not. Crafted Dragon Lord gear needs proficiency 55, is Unique, and has one more Soul Binding line than dungeon drops (CRAFTING-RESEARCH.md sections 4 to 8).
  - Blacksmithing: weapons and guards. Armorsmithing: armour. Handicrafting: jewellery, bows and staffs. Alchemy: potions, scrolls, Manastones and Soulstones. Cooking: buff food.
- Lv 35 and up: build the Unique pieces, one at a time.

### Gate 2: the grade
- Unique or better: a keeper. Follow the steps below.
- Epic, Rare, Common: do not enhance it, not even with cheap stones. Replace it with a Unique piece (from a drop or a craft).

### Gate 3: which piece first
- The weapon, then the guard, then the armour, then the accessories.

### The steps for each Unique piece, in order
1. Growth to +10: every step succeeds; extraction gives the stones back.
2. Past +10: the weapon, then the guard only; one step at a time; the failure bonus caps the tries.
3. Soul Binding: reroll weak lines for the role first, then Bind towards 100%.
4. Manastones: top stones for the role in every slot (keepers only; Transfer loses them).
5. Theostone: the weapon you keep, last.
6. Transfer: when a better Unique drops for the slot, move the enhance level and Soul Binding across, then rebuild stones and Theostone.

### What the tool does with it
- Each piece's What to do follows the gates: below Lv 35 a non-Unique piece says "wear it, do not enhance it"; from Lv 35 it says "replace it with a Unique piece". No manastone step on non-Unique gear.
- The Enhance tab shows the gates with the Daeva's own stage lit, the steps, and below Lv 35 the prepare path (gather and craft).
