# Gladiator: tank with damage (research record, 17 Oct 2026)

Miguel's main. His role in his words (17 Oct 2026): a tank build with a DPS element. Keep aggro, keep damage uptime on Keen Strike and Overhead Slam, buff the team, and use parry to boost team damage.
Status per fact: C = confirmed by a source quoting tooltips, R = reported or one guide's advice, U = unknown. Sources in the repo only, never on a page.

## 1. What the role is in a group
- Guides class the Gladiator as an off-tank or bruiser; the Templar is the main tank (R). Without a Templar the Gladiator can main tank, holding aggro through steady damage.
- No taunt (C). Enmity comes from damage and from Keen Strike, Rending Blow and Overhead Slam (C); Rending Blow raises Enmity directly (C). No enmity values published (U).
- So for this role, damage uptime IS the aggro tool. That is why Keen Strike and Overhead Slam uptime matter.

## 2. The team-damage tools
- **Focused Block** (stigma, 20 s, held) (C): blocks and parries frontal hits; each parry gives 110 MP and stamina; each block takes 0.1 s off its cooldown. Specialty: 10% HP on parry. Extra charge at stigma Lv 5 (R). Some boss attacks go through it (R).
- **Experienced Counterstrike** (passive) (C): raises your front attack damage; a successful block gives the party +9%, 13% or 17% PvE damage for 20 s by passive rank. Needs you facing the boss (R).
- **It does not stack with the Templar's Fury** (party +10 to 20%) (C). With a Templar in the group, your parry buff is wasted.
- **Wave Armor** (stigma, 60 s, 20 s) (C): damage over time on 4 targets; specialty multiplies Experienced Counterstrike by 1.5 (also Murderous Burst x1.5, or 10% damage reflect).
- **Zikel's Blessing** (120 s): Attack +20%, Accuracy +100 for 10 s (C). **Rage Burst** (45 s, 30 s at Lv 5): Wounded, +10% PvE damage, opens Overhead Slam 10 s (C).

## 3. Uptime tools
- **Keen Strike** (main attack, no cooldown) (C): +100 MP, Enmity; specialties +20% MP, 1% HP leech, +50% Multi-Hit, minus 1 s Ruinous Blow per hit (Lv 12), Reckless Strike chain (Lv 16).
- **Overhead Slam** (5 s) (C): needs a knocked-down target (7% proc otherwise). Windows: Rage Burst 10 s, Mocking Blade knockdown 3 s (+1 s specialty), Rush Strike 30% knockdown. Specialties: Upward Strike (Lv 8), always crits (Lv 12), no cooldown (Lv 16, picked by hand). Whether Lv 16 still needs the window is U.
- **Rending Blow** (no cooldown, 120 MP) (C): Enmity, +200 Block while using it (Lv 8 option), 50 MP back on a crit (Lv 20).
- **Ankle Slice**: readied by a parry (from our data); cooldown U.

## 4. Defensives (own keys) (C)
Tenaciousness (150 s: 3 s immunity, then +50% PvE damage tolerance 3 s), Defiance (60 s, CC break). Passives: Survival Stance, Protection Armor (Block, heal on block), Blood Absorption, Survival Willpower.

For Miguel's tank-with-damage role:
- Same main line and macro. Hold Keen Strike with it for aggro and MP (his goal is Keen Strike uptime); test on the dummy whether the macro alone or with Keen Strike holds aggro better.
- Main tank with no Templar: Wave Armor (x1.5 Counterstrike) replaces Lunge Stance on key 6.
- Parry with Focused Block on boss swings every 20 s for the party buff.

Opener: Leaping Slam in, Focused Block on the first frontal swing (party buff), Zikel's Blessing and Lunge Stance (or Wave Armor), Ruinous Blow, then hold the macro.

Specialties: Overhead Slam Upward Strike, then crit, then no cooldown; Keen Strike +50% Multi-Hit, then minus 1 s Ruinous Blow; Rending Blow +200 Block, then MP on crit; Focused Block 10% HP on parry.

## 6. Daevanion inside 415 points
- Season 1 cap is 415 points (questlog.gg builds); the five boards cost 802, so no player completes them all (C).
- Most-used by Gladiators (metabot, nodes not points): Nezekan 63 of 88, Zikel 56 of 88, Vaizel 52 of 88, Triniel 42 of 116, Azphel 3 of 152 (C).
- Public 415 Gladiator build (questlog, grobmotoriker): Nezekan 97, Zikel 103, Vaizel 94, Triniel 121, Azphel 0, giving +4 to the core skills and passives (C for that build).
- Daevanion gives up to +4 skill levels, so Mastery 11 + 4 = 15: Lv 16 specialties need +1 from gear (C).
- Node priority for this role (R): Overhead Slam, Rending Blow, Ruinous Blow, Keen Strike +1; then Experienced Counterstrike and Attack Preparation +1; then both Damage Tolerance nodes; then Defence (Justice, Nezekan) and HP; then Multi-Hit; then Cooldown Reduction. Block nodes on Gladiator boards: U.
- How to follow it in game (fits Miguel's "one board at a time"): work one board at a time in route order, but a board is "done for your role" once its nodes on this list are taken. Then move to the next board. Never spend on the rest of a board while a better node waits on the next.

## 7. Stats
Main: Dexterity (Block, Evasion, Crit Resist) first, then Might, Constitution, Precision (R). Each point +0.1% (C). Sub-stats: Front Attack Damage Boost, Block, Accuracy, Damage Tolerance. Manastone caps: HP +160, Defence +100, Block +20, Attack +10 (C). Armour: Block and HP; weapon: Attack.

## 8. In-game checks for Miguel
1. Lv 16 Overhead Slam no-cooldown: does it fire on a dummy with no knockdown?
2. The parry buff in the party bar: its number and 20 s length; with a Templar, do the two stack?
3. Ankle Slice: lights only after a parry?
4. Does the main line skip a greyed Overhead Slam and fire Rending Blow?
5. Do any of your boards have Block nodes?
6. Holding aggro with no taunt against a strong DPS for 60 s.
