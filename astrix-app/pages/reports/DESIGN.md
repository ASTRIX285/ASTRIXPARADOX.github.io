# Reports design direction

Scope: Reports only. Preserve the surrounding ASTRIX identity and the existing three-stage information architecture.

## Direction

A compact activity archive for Destiny players. Activity selection art supplies identity; the interface supplies clear grouping, alignment and contrast. Shared ASTRIX canvas, panel, text, muted, action and focus tokens remain authoritative. No new brand palette or imagery.

## First viewport

Reports title and character selector occupy one toolbar. Activity families sit in a horizontal navigation strip. The selected series has a short heading above a responsive gallery of image-and-name cards. Cards have left-aligned titles anchored to the artwork, with no totals or promotional copy inside them.

## Activity analysis

The left column groups selection art, totals and exact difficulty/character tables. The right column has difficulty controls and date/result rows. Rows share a continuous ruled list, with alternating surfaces. Supporting definitions are available through native disclosure controls.

## Run report

A centred report is capped at 1280px. A prominent 320px activity-art header (260px on mobile) sits above the fireteam table. Body cells use 14px type, headers 13px and run metadata 16px. Strong opaque table surfaces, column rules, aligned numerals and subdued alternate rows keep the background from competing with data. The full table scrolls inside its own container on mobile. Player links remain explicit.

## Interaction and states

Selecting a left difficulty row highlights both the row and its matching right-side control. Completion labels retain their full words; a filled or hollow marker adds scanability without replacing the text. Controls use a 44px baseline target, visible keyboard focus and short state transitions. Reduced-motion preference removes transitions and art zoom. No entrance choreography.

## Type and spacing

Use the incumbent Bahnschrift family with sans-serif fallback. Fixed heading sizes, tabular numerals, a 3px corner radius and 12/16/20/24/32px spacing establish a consistent scale. Keep explanatory copy subordinate to actual results.

## Verification limits

Synthetic fixture previews are not live account evidence. Browser validation must be reported separately from data and static validators; never claim a visual pass when the browser cannot render the branch.
