Destiny 2 build advisor

Most build tools show you what to equip. ASTRIX PARADOX explains why it works: how your subclass, weapons, armour and mods feed each other, with every claim traced back to Bungie's own data.

Enter The ForgeExplore the DIM loadout viewer

From your Guardian to your next build

Start with your career, inspect a shared loadout or build around the gear you have. Connect Bungie when you enter the tools.

Guardian Home

See your time played, class breakdown and career highlights from Bungie data. Your latest activity gives you a starting point for the next session.

Forge Loader

Choose an Exotic armour piece, an optional Exotic weapon, a set bonus and a stat focus. Compare ranked armour combinations in the Forge Matrix before sending a selection to Builder.

Builder

Choose an element, a build objective and an activity for your Working Build. Review recommendations and the Bungie descriptions behind supported connections between your Exotic, subclass and other components.

DIM loadout import

Open a shared DIM link to inspect its subclass, weapons, armour and mods. The import preserves the shared original and can compare it with matches or suggested replacements from your inventory.

Storage

Browse your Vault, carried gear and Postmaster items. Inspect items and move gear between your Vault and Guardians.

Reports

Choose an activity, review its difficulty breakdown and browse your run history. Open a run to see its fireteam results from Bungie's activity report.

Already have a DIM build?

Keep DIM in your workflow. Bring a shared loadout into ASTRIX PARADOX to inspect its pieces and explore how they fit together.
