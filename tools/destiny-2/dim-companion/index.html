A companion, not a replacement

Looking for a DIM alternative? Start with the job you need done. DIM brings inventory management, transfers, saved loadouts and armour optimisation together. ASTRIX PARADOX adds a place to inspect a shared build, review supported synergies and look back at your Guardian's career and activities.

These are useful starting points, not a complete feature checklist. The two projects are independent.
